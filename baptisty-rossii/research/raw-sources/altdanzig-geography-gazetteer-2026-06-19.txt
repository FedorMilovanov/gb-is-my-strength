Source B: migrationsmuseum.it / migrazioni.altervista.org "Altdanzig".
URL: http://www.migrazioni.altervista.org/deu/3deutsche_in_russland/05_18jahrhundert/altdanzig.html
Verified 2026-06-19. Key facts (German):
- Die evangelische Mutterkolonie Altdanzig (auch: Danzig, heute: ukrainisch Крупське/Krupske; russisch Крупское/Krupskoje) liegt etwa 15 km suedwestlich von Kirowograd am Ufer des Suhoklija (Сугоклія), einem 44 km langen Nebenfluss des Inhul.
- Gegruendet 1787 von 29 protestantischen Familien aus dem Regierungsbezirk Danzig im Koenigreich Preussen.
- In den 1920er Jahren wurde Altdanzig in Karliwka (Карлівка) und schliesslich zu Ehren der Ehefrau Lenins, Nadeschda Krupskaja, in Krupske umbenannt.

CROSS-CHECK with Dorodnitsyn (research file 38 / raw doc excerpts):
- "крестьянин Ефим Цимбал, 11 июня 1869 принял крещение в реке Сугаклея у немецкой колонии Старый Данциг; крестил Абрам Унгер из Старой Хортицы близ Кичкаса."
- The river name (Сугаклея ~ Suhoklija/Сугоклія) and colony (Старый Данциг = Alt-Danzig = Krupske) match independently. This raises the Tsymbal/Unger 1869 baptism site to A- geographic confidence.

NAME-CONFUSION CAUTION:
- Alt-Danzig was renamed Karlivka (Карлівка) in the 1920s, then Krupske.
- This is DIFFERENT from "дер. Карловка" mentioned in the 1884 Novo-Vasilyevka protocol as the home village of delegate Timofei Khistun. Do not conflate the two "Karlovka/Karlivka" without page-level verification.

--- NOVOVASYLIVKA DISAMBIGUATION (verified 2026-06-19) ---
There are TWO villages "Novovasylivka" in Zaporizhzhia oblast. Use the BERDIANSK RAION one for the 1884 congress.
- Novovasylivka, BERDIANSK Raion (historical, Tavrida gub., Berdiansk uyezd): mindat feature 699058 = 46.85665 N, 36.77933 E (alt names: Kengez, Novo-Vasil'yevsk). Matches V.G. Pavlov biography "с. Нововасильевка, Бердянского уезда, Таврической губернии". -> 1884 Baptist Union congress site.
- Novovasylivka, MELITOPOL Raion (DIFFERENT village): 46.83500 N, 35.74778 E. Do NOT use for 1884.

--- LYUBOMIRKA CAUTION ---
"Lyubomirka" is a common toponym. maplandia lists one in Khmelnytska oblast (50.0333 N, 27.6667 E) - almost certainly NOT Ryaboshapka's. Ryaboshapka's Lyubomirka was in the Kherson/Elisavetgrad zone (today Kirovohrad oblast). Needs GeoNames + pre-revolutionary map cross-check before a coordinate is asserted. Mark as warn/unresolved.
