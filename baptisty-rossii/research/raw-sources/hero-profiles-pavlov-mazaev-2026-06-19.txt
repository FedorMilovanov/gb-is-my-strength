Hero profiles: Pavlov & Mazaev. Verified 2026-06-19. Reader text per editorial policy.

=== V. G. PAVLOV (Wikipedia/Ruwiki; baptistru.info; baptist.org.ru/1422402) ===
"Василий Гурьевич Павлов (1 февраля 1854 - 14 апреля 1924) - российский миссионер и проповедник, один из основателей и первых руководителей Союза русских баптистов."
- Born Feb 1854, s. Vorontsovka (sources differ: Шемахинская / Александропольский уезд Эриванской / Тифлисской губ.), Molokan family (settlers from Ryazan province).
- 1871: in Tiflis joined the Baptist community, baptized by N. I. Voronin (rusbaptist: 9 April 1871, among first ~10 members; age 17).
- 1875: sent by Tiflis church to Hamburg; Oncken's seminary already disbanded due to conflict among German Baptists -> Oncken appointed preacher Peter Vilrat (Wilrath) to teach Pavlov theology & German; one-year course.
- 1876: ordained missionary by Oncken; returned to Tiflis; translated the Hamburg Confession of Oncken into Russian (long the ECB confession).
- 1879: baptized converts in Vladikavkaz (founded a church) -> arrested; elected presbyter of Tiflis by secret ballot.
- 1880: ordained presbyter (by Liebig of Odessa and Kargel of Petersburg); with Wieler preached in Novo-Vasilyevka, Astrakhanka, Novo-Spasskoye (Berdyansk uyezd).
- 1882: at Rückenau conference elected Caucasus missionary on full support.
- 1884: at the St Petersburg united congress (Pashkov/Korf).
- 1887 & 1891: two 4-year exiles to Orenburg; 1892 wife and three children died (cholera/drowning); only son Pavel survived; 1893 second marriage.
- 1895: emigrated, pastor in Tulcea (Romania/now Bulgaria); 1901 returned to Tiflis.
- 1905 London + 1911 Philadelphia BWA congresses. 1907 redaktsiya of "Baptist"; 1908 deputy chairman of Union; 1909 (Rostov-on-Don congress) elected chairman of Russian Baptist Union; 1910-1911 editor-in-chief of "Baptist".
- Polyglot: priest Brikhnichev listed ~25 languages; Pavlov himself: "о Христе простыми словами могу сказать на 24 языках; пишу на 7." "более сорока раз арестовывался."
- Predecessor as chairman: D. I. Mazaev; successor: I. A. Golyaev. Son: Pavel Vasilyevich Pavlov.

=== D. I. MAZAEV (Pravenc text/2561218; baptistru.info; Wikipedia) ===
Pravenc: "Дей Иванович (1855, с. Ново-Васильевка Бердянского у. Таврической губ. - 20.05.1922, Моздок ...), баптист. пресвитер. Один из основателей Союза русских баптистов (1884). В 1888-1917 гг. неоднократно избирался его председателем. Создатель и 1-й редактор ж. 'Баптист' (1907-1909, 1912, 1917 - нач. 1918)."
- Family of Molokans exiled to Tavria for leaving Orthodoxy; after exile, 1867 resettled to Kuban; sheep-breeding.
- 11 Nov 1884: M. and younger brother Gavriil with wives baptized in r. Kavalerka by presbyter of Novo-Vasilyevka community V. R. Kolodin; father expelled him from home, threatening disinheritance.
- 1885: elected presbyter; 1886 (3rd congress) deputy chairman; 4th congress (Dec 1887-Jan 1888) chairman of Russian Baptist Union, re-elected to 1909 (~25 years); also led Union in 1917.
- 1896: father died, large fortune; M. bred the "mazaevskaya" sheep breed ("знатный овцевод России"; nicknamed "русский Соломон"). Funded Union, "Baptist" journal, Prokhanov's "Beseda"; built prayer house in Nakhichevan-on-Don (1915).
- 1905 London BWA congress with V. V. Ivanov, V. G. Pavlov, V. A. Fetler (translator) - BWA founded.
- Supported union with Evangelical Christians and the name "Evangelical Christians-Baptists."
- Dec 1917, Khasavyurt: defending home/family from robbers, accidentally killed an attacker; local community excommunicated him, later restored after sincere repentance.
- Predecessor as chairman: I. I. Wieler; successor (1909, after Mazaev's self-withdrawal): V. G. Pavlov. Brother: Gavriil Ivanovich Mazaev. A. M. Mazaev (addressee of Voronin's 1889 letter, file 30) was a relative.

CAUTIONS: Pavlov birth-province varies across sources (QA); "24 languages" partly legendary; Mazaev 1917 killing is documented (Pravenc) - present with context (self-defense, repentance, restoration), not sensational.
