--- Poysti (Wikipedia / kniga.org.ua / bogopoznanie / baptist.org.ru "75 лет РХР" / BBN Russian) ---
Nikolai Ivanovich Poysti: born St Petersburg (aristocratic family; Swedish father, mother of Olenin line); converted at 15 at home meetings in Princess Lieven's house (fruit of Radstock revival); preached in Petersburg, exiled by Okhranka; missionary Primorye/Harbin; in US led European-Eastern Mission; journal "Put' very"; began Russian radio with son 1946; died Nov 1947.
Yarl (Earl) Nikolaevich Poysti (9 Oct 1920 - 8 Mar 2010): born Russian Far East (Nikolsk-Ussuriysk); converted age 14 in Harbin (1935); USA, National Bible Institute (1943), New York University; ordained; after father's death (1947) led the radio ministry 50+ years. Broadcast via IBRA-Radio (Sweden), Trans World Radio (Monte Carlo), from Austria, Sweden, Colorado; up to 25 hrs/week from 12 stations (Monaco, Portugal, Saipan). 1961 trip to USSR (Russia, Ukraine) with Jack Wyrtzen ("Word of Life") -> youth program. Slogan "Millions of Russians for Christ". After 1991, son John Poysti founded Russian Christian Radio in Moscow (Radio-1, Mayak, Yunost, Radio Rossii). Principle: cooperation with all born-again Christians regardless of denomination ("the spirit of sectarianism was foreign to him").
