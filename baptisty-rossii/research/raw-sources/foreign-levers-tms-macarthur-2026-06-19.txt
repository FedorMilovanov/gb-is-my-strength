Foreign levers: TMS/MacArthur/TMAI. Verified 2026-06-19. Reader text per editorial policy.

--- TMAI origins (jmacarthurtrust.org/the-masters-academy-international) ---
"The roots of TMAI go back to 1992 when leaders of the Baptist Union from Ukraine first approached John MacArthur and Grace Church to request help in training their pastors. A team of missionaries - all graduates of The Master's Seminary - was sent to provide the requested training. In the ensuing years, similar requests came from numerous other countries... Today, TMAI is comprised of 15 international training centers..."

--- Irpin (tmai.org/update/tmai-professors-honored-by-ukrainian-leadership/) ---
"After communism fell in 1990... the leadership at Irpin Biblical Seminary reached out to The Master's Seminary for help in training church leaders in the region. In 1991-2 missionaries were sent out to establish what would become the first TMAI training center, and a global movement of pastoral training was born."

--- TMAI history (tmai.org/stories/read/history-of-tmai; /about/history; tmai.events) ---
"In 2004, the ministry was officially founded, with ten training centers represented." Now 18-19 centers, 7,500-8,000+ graduates. Member schools include Irpin Biblical Seminary (Kyiv) and Samara (Russia); EBTC Berlin hub.
Distinctive: students trained in their own countries; centers handed to indigenous leaders.

--- Samara Center for Biblical Training (tmai.org/schools/scbt; samaracbt.com) ---
"Our training center was founded in 2000 when pastor of Transfiguration Baptist Church extended an invitation to Grace Community Church to help with training Russian pastors and preachers. The first group started their Level 1 training in October 2000. Then seminars in Level 2 program began in the Fall 2002."
Formats: Level 1 (expository preaching, 2 yrs, modular); Level 2 full-time (residential, 3 yrs, M.Div-like: biblical languages, exegesis, theology, church history, pastoral); Level 2 part-time (4 yrs).
SCBT = ministry of Transfiguration Church of Samara; serving churches 20+ years (Irkutsk, Balashov, etc.).

--- Alexey Prokopenko (tmai.org/update/good-news-from-russia/) ---
"Alexey Prokopenko has served as a professor at Samara Center for Biblical Training since 2007 and as pastor of Transfiguration Baptist Church in Samara, Russia, since 2012. He has earned degrees from Medical University of Samara (MD) and The Master's Seminary (PhD). Alexey has authored several books in the Russian language, including commentaries on Genesis and Ruth, as well as annotated translations of Proverbs and Ecclesiastes."

--- Word of Grace Bible Institute (wgbc.org/history-detailed; tmai.org/school/usa-russian/) ---
"In January of 2001 the Biblical Institute 'Word Of Grace' at Slavic Baptist Church based in Vancouver began its work. Men from Masters Seminary conducted monthly sessions, inviting church ministers from Oregon and Washington states." Goal: train Russian-speaking ministers.

--- Grace to You / GRO (gty.org/translations; jmacarthurtrust.org/people/gro) ---
"We are now also translating John's sermons into Italian, Romanian, and Russian..." GRO: ~600 sermons (100 series) per language. MacArthur Study Bible available in Russian (and Spanish, German, French, Portuguese, Italian, Arabic, Chinese).
Cold War: "Throughout the Cold War, his sermons had been broadcast into Russia and had led many men and women to faith in Christ. His expository preaching through Grace to You had paved the way for these missionaries..."
