"my first trip which was to kiev which is right now as i'm speaking to you being bombed and i've been all through that city many many times many of the most wonderful pastors conferences i've ever been a part of in my entire life were in kiev the central church in kiev i was there i was in kiev when the soviet union really collapsed and i was there on a day when they went through the main square ... that main square had statues of [Lenin] everywhere but when the breakup came i was there when they built scaffolds around the statues of [Lenin] and took sledgehammers and smashed [Lenin] to the ground i watched them knock down statue after statue ... when i was there that was euphoric freedom"

--- ADDITIONAL (transcript chunks 3-4), verified 2026-06-19 ---
Mark Tatlock (TMAI president): "my first exposure to the church in the former soviet union was a trip i took in 1990." The idea of training centers came from the Baptist Union leaders in those countries (~1990-1992), who "were looking for those they could trust" and focused on MacArthur (they had read his book "The Charismatics"). "TMAI's first training center was launched in Ukraine ... that was the genesis of what's now the Master's Academy International." Today ~18 member schools; ~10 led by nationals.
Narrator: MacArthur "has gone to multiple cities to preach in Russian churches and to gather the Russian pastors together." MacArthur: packed churches - "you can't see the carpet in a russian church ... they're sitting and standing and jammed to the walls ... windows open even in the winter and they're standing outside."
Antioch Initiative (founded by Bob Provost) in YAKUTIA (Far East Russia, "the size of India but with only one million people"): training first-generation believer pastors (former paganism - "lucky charms faith ... sun moon stars"), all given MacArthur Study Bibles; one now at Novosibirsk seminary studying for M.Div.
Radio/print scale: MacArthur Study Bible 5 print runs in Russian; ~370,000 MacArthur books distributed; 100+ Russian pastors come to Shepherds' Conference yearly.
Bruce Alvard (with Greg White and Brian Kinsel) taught for ~30 years -> "hundreds and hundreds of these russian pastors and ukrainian pastors"; one TMAI student's city "Diemer" (transcription of IRPIN) overtaken by Russian troops in 2022.
