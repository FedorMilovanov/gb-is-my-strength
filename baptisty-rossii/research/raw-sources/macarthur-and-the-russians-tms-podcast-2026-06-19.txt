--- MacArthur's own words (verbatim from transcript) ---
"my first trip which was to kiev which is right now as i'm speaking to you being bombed and i've been all through that city many many times many of the most wonderful pastors conferences i've ever been a part of in my entire life were in kiev the central church in kiev i was there i was in kiev when the soviet union really collapsed and i was there on a day when they went through the main square ... that main square had statues of [Lenin] everywhere but when the breakup came i was there when they built scaffolds around the statues of [Lenin] and took sledgehammers and smashed [Lenin] to the ground i watched them knock down statue after statue ... when i was there that was euphoric freedom"
[Narrator: 1991 trip; "that trip changed John MacArthur and ... how Grace Community Church and the Master's Seminary go about missions"; "over the past 30 years John's influence on world missions is best seen in the slavic world in russia ukraine and throughout the former soviet union."]

--- 1991 resurfaced interview (MacArthur, in Ukraine) ---
"today i talked to a ukrainian television crew that are making a documentary on sin and redemption ... they said how does one have faith in god and what is the gospel and i explained the gospel..."

--- Radio into USSR from early 1970s (MacArthur, 1975 sermon "Christians and Social Revolution") ---
"the messages that we give you here are now being translated into russian first and second peter to be broadcast into russia ... between 14 and 20 million people ... listening to an all christian radio station ... beaming in ... the russian government can't afford to stop it or block it it's outside the country ... that conversation ... was probably the entry point ... that was in the early 70s i was aware of the growing church but in those days it was an underground church"

--- SGA / Provost / Omelchenko (transcript) ---

MacArthur: "one of the early conferences in kiev my translator was sergei omelchenko ... who actually has translated my commentaries into russian through the years." "he came back saying if he could speak russian he'd move there. 10 conferences MacArthur had." "we translated the study bible and did five print runs ... MacArthur books 370,000 copies ... distributed to the r[ussian-speaking world]."
