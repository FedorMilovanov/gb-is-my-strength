Foreign channels: SGA / radio / Bible smuggling. Verified 2026-06-19. Reader text per editorial policy.

--- SGA (sga.org/our-history; sgacanada.ca; Wheaton Records; MinistryWatch; CT 1991) ---
"...founded by Rev. Peter Deyneka, an immigrant from Belarus... In the 1920s Peter traveled throughout the Soviet Union... When ... Stalin intensified persecution of the churches in the early 1930s... he and a small group of Chicago-area businessmen met in the back of Hedstrom's Shoe Store and founded the Russian Gospel Association in 1934. It was later renamed the Slavic Gospel Association (SGA). For many decades, SGA covertly distributed millions of Bibles and Christian books under the Iron Curtain... while producing and broadcasting thousands of Christian radio programs..."
Wheaton: incorporated as Russian Gospel Association March 1936; renamed SGA 1949. Founders: Peter Deyneka, George Benson, Arthur I. Brown, C. B. Hedstrom, Paul W. Rood. HQ Chicago -> Wheaton (1975). 1941: Deyneka talked with Clarence Jones at HCJB about shortwave to USSR; broadcasts began over HCJB; later via Trans-World Radio (Monaco), Far East Broadcasting (Philippines), KLKX (Korea), KICY (Alaska). 1975: ~600 broadcasts/month. Russian Bible Institute: Benito Manitoba 1941 -> Toronto 1943 (People's Church, Oswald J. Smith).
Peter Deyneka Jr. (1931-2000): Wheaton College 1953, M.Div Northern Baptist Seminary 1957; president SGA 1975-1991; denied Soviet visas 13 years (1976-1989); translated ~100 book titles. SGA closely tied to UECB (Union of Evangelical Christians-Baptists). 1991: Deynekas left SGA, formed Peter Deyneka USSR Ministries (Russian Ministries).
Today SGA works in Russia, Ukraine, Belarus, Georgia, Kazakhstan, Uzbekistan, Tajikistan, Turkmenistan, Azerbaijan, Armenia.

--- HCJB / SonSet Solutions ---
HCJB Quito Ecuador from 1931 (Clarence Jones, Reuben Larson). "build a 500,000-watt shortwave transmitter capable of punching through any Russian jamming effort." Project Outreach / HC500 completed 1980, broadcasting into USSR. Reports: Soviet border guards and a jamming-station operator became believers. 1986 World by 2000 challenge.

--- Trans World Radio (Wikipedia; Shortwave Central) ---
Paul Freed founded TWR (1952 Voice of Tangier -> TWR). Contract with Radio Monte Carlo Sept 1959; Monte Carlo station inaugurated 16 October 1960 in the former Nazi transmitter building on Mt Agel (100 kW; +100 kW 1961; 500 kW 1982). Broadcast into Soviet bloc; now largest Christian media org (190+ countries, 230+ languages).

--- Bible smuggling (Hungarian Review; Cambridge "Christian Outlaws"; CHVN; LA Times 1988) ---
Brother Andrew (Anne/Andrew van der Bijl): God's Smuggler (1967); Open Doors (founded after 1955 Poland trip; Romania crossing 1957; blue VW Beetle). Richard Wurmbrand (Romanian): Tortured for Christ; Jesus to the Communist World -> Voice of the Martyrs. Other groups: Underground Evangelism (L. Joe Bass), Misjon bak Jernteppet (Norway), Finnish Lutheran Mission, Glaube in der 2. Welt (Switzerland), Danish European Mission, Door of Hope (Seattle). Keston College (Michael Bourdeaux, John Lawrence) - research center on religion & communism; KGB considered it one of the most dangerous anti-Soviet orgs. Open Doors infiltrated by KGB informers.
1988 (glasnost): USSR accepted offers of ~2 million New Testaments from Open Doors + Door of Hope; first 100,000 from Open Doors arrived in Moscow; Karchev (Council for Religious Affairs): "If you can provide us with another million Bibles, please come with them."
