## Editorial boundary

The parent file is an interpretive thesis, not a self-sufficient factual source. Its claims may be used in publication only when the article also cites the underlying imperial, Baptist, Orthodox-polemical, academic, or Soviet-period evidence listed below. The word “sect” changed meaning between imperial and Soviet settings; continuity of vocabulary must not be presented as continuity of an identical legal or theological category.
