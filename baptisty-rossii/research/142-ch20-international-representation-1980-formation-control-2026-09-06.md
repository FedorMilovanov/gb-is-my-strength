## 0. Why this file exists

A common later biographical formula says:

> `В 1979 году Георгий Винс основал зарубежное Представительство Совета церквей ЕХБ.`

That chronology is attractive because Vins arrived in the United States in Apr 1979 and immediately began international testimony.

But a contemporary Baptist news report from **10 Jul 1980** says that **earlier in 1980** Vins formed the International Representation for the Council of Evangelical Baptist Churches in the Soviet Union, with offices in Elkhart, Indiana.

This contemporary witness is temporally much closer to the organizational event than later biographies.

Therefore `1979 founding` can no longer be repeated as canonical without an actual incorporation/founding document.

---

# A. CONTEMPORARY 1980 WITNESS

## 1. Southern Baptist news service, 10 Jul 1980

A contemporary Southern Baptist news-service issue reports in the context of the Baptist World Alliance / Soviet Baptist dispute:

> earlier that year Vins formed the **International Representation for the Council of Evangelical Baptist Churches in the Soviet Union**, with offices in **Elkhart, Indiana**.

The same report says the organization was sponsoring the `Voice of the Persecuted Church` meeting.

### Evidence value

This is not the organization's own incorporation certificate.

But it is:
- contemporary;
- institutionally close to the Baptist world in which the new representation was operating;
- explicit about formation timing (`earlier this year`);
- explicit about name and Elkhart office.

Current classification:
- **A2/B1 contemporaneous institutional-news witness**, depending local taxonomy application;
- formation year **1980 STRONG**;
- exact legal incorporation date **OPEN**.

# C. 1979 — TESTIMONY BEFORE ORGANIZATION

## 3. Vins already had an international public role in 1979

The correction to 1980 does **not** mean Vins was internationally inactive in 1979.

Closed 1979 activities include:
- U.S. arrival after forced expulsion/exchange;
- Senate guest prayer;
- CSCE/Helsinki Commission testimony, 7 Jun 1979;
- extensive church/public speaking;
- international advocacy around Soviet religious persecution.

Therefore Chapter 20 should distinguish:

```text

Vins as expelled Soviet Baptist witness / speaker / advocate
        ↓
1980
formal International Representation organizational lane
```

This distinction is historically stronger than backdating the organization to the moment of his arrival.

# D. LATER `1979 FOUNDED` CLAIM

## 4. Why it is downgraded

Later evangelical/encyclopedic biographies often say:
- Vins founded the representation in 1979;
- sometimes simply `after arriving in America`.

Possible explanations include:
- retrospective compression of arrival + advocacy + later organization;
- preparatory activity in 1979 before formal organization;
- an incorporation/founding event not yet found that predates the July-1980 news wording.

None should be guessed.

Current verdict:

> **`International Representation founded in 1979` = HOLD / DO NOT CANONICALIZE.**

If exact corporate records later show a 1979 filing, reopen this conclusion and explain why the July 1980 report describes formation as `earlier this year`.
