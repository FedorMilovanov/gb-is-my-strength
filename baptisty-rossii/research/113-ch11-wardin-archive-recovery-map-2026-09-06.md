For every received scan/PDF:
1. preserve original filename and archive citation;
2. record collection/box/folder;
3. record page/image count;
4. compute SHA256;
5. visually inspect pages;
6. identify dates/authors/recipients;
7. map exact claim → page;
8. separate originals from later Wardin notes/clippings;
9. classify rights/reproduction restrictions;

## 8. Questions the archive should answer

1. What exactly did Neprash ask for in Dec1921 and to whom?
2. Are the 1922 donation reports cumulative or period-specific?
3. Which remittances actually reached Russia and through what mechanism?
4. Did ARA directly handle the Russian-Ukrainian Baptist union's transfers, or only some of them?
5. What did Everett Gill distribute, where, and under whose local supervision?
6. Who sat on the all-Russian Baptist-Evangelical relief committee?
7. Which relief reached non-Baptists?
8. What did Rushbrooke report privately versus publicly?
9. Were foreign grants formally conditioned on a joint Russian committee/union, and if so which grants?
10. Which money was famine relief, which was Bible Fund, which was College Fund, which was general mission support?
