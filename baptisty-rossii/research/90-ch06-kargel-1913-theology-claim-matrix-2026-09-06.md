## 1. Structure of the 1913 confession

The brochure is organized in ten sections:

1. О Боге;
2. О Слове Божием;
3. О человеке вообще;
4. О спасении человека;
5. О Церкви Христовой;
6. О служителях Христовых и Церкви;
7. Об установлениях Христовых для Церкви;
8. О браке;
9. О государственной власти;
10. О втором пришествии Христа и суде Его.

This structure itself is useful for the chapter: Kargel's theology is not only a spirituality of personal experience; it includes Scripture, anthropology, soteriology, ecclesiology, ministry, ordinances, family ethics, civil authority and eschatology.

## 3. High-value Chapter 6 arguments now supportable

### A. `Bridge` was theological, not only geographical

Kargel's life connected places and networks, but the 1913 document shows a second kind of connection: he offered a compact theological grammar capable of speaking about Scripture, salvation, church, ministry and civil authority in one coherent frame.

This is stronger than a travel-biography chapter.

### B. Unity must not be collapsed into organization

The confession speaks strongly about believers of different peoples and conditions being one body in Christ, while also giving local churches concrete functions of worship, formation, service, mission and discipline.

Therefore Chapter 6 can show why Kargel belongs between the Petersburg narrative and the later Mazaev/Prokhanov organizational comparison without claiming that his ecclesiology simply demanded one administrative union.

### C. The state/conscience theme already exists before the Soviet era

The civil-authority section combines strong obedience language with an explicit boundary where allegiance to God cannot be displaced by human interference.

This gives Chapter 13 (`Совесть, армия и государство`) a legitimate backward cross-reference, but Chapter 6 should not import later Soviet conflicts into 1913.

### D. Baptismal identity is explicit

Whatever the unresolved biographical question of who baptized Kargel in 1869, his own 1913 confession clearly describes baptism as following preaching/instruction, repentance and faith and excludes infant baptism.

This separates a disputed biographical detail from an undisputed theological position.

## 4. What NOT to say

- `Каргель создал богословие всех русских баптистов` — too broad.
- `Вероучение 1913 было официальным вероучением всего движения` — false for its original context.
- `Каргель был просто арминианином` — label requires comparative analysis; the primary text should be described before classification.
- `Вечеря у Каргеля только символическая` — text also uses participation/communion language.
- `Каргель учил безусловному подчинению государству` — incomplete; his section contains an explicit God/Caesar boundary.
- `1913 автоматически объясняет 1944/1966` — teleology.

## 5. Reception layer — separate chronology

The fact that ВСЕХБ later adopted/used Kargel's confession belongs to reception history, not to the original 1913 object.

A 1966 `Братский Вестник` publication provides a strong later institutional witness that ВСЕХБ leadership proposed Kargel's confession and explicitly cited its brevity/clarity and his standing among Baptists and Evangelical Christians.

That material is valuable as a restrained epilogue:

`текст, созданный в конкретной петербургской среде 1913 года, десятилетия спустя получил новую общесоюзную функцию`.

Do not invert that chronology.
