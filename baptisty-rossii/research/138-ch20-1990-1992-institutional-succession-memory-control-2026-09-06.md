## 0. Why this file exists

The end of a Soviet all-Union church body is easy to oversimplify:

> `СССР распался в декабре 1991 → ВСЕХБ исчез → появился Российский союз → затем Евро-Азиатская федерация.`

This compresses four different processes:
1. internal church constitutional reform;
2. legal change in freedom-of-conscience regime;
3. collapse of the Soviet federal state and emergence of sovereign republics;
4. creation/reconfiguration of national and transnational Baptist bodies.

The dates overlap but are not identical.

---

# A. TERMINOLOGY CONTROL

## 1. Do not use `ВСЕХБ` as an unchanging name through 1992

By the 44th all-Union congress in Feb 1990 the official church publication presents a new charter and a reorganized `Союз евангельских христиан-баптистов СССР / Союз ЕХБ` model.

Therefore late-Soviet prose must distinguish:
- historical `ВСЕХБ` governance model;
- 1990 `Союз ЕХБ` charter/structure;
- republican/national Baptist unions;
- post-Soviet Euro-Asian Federation.

Do not mechanically call every 1990–1992 body `ВСЕХБ` merely because later history treats it as organizational succession.

# G. SUCCESSOR LANGUAGE

## 13. `Правопреемник` needs a source class

Some later histories call the Euro-Asian Federation the successor to ВСЕХБ/Union EHB.

This can mean several things:
- theological/historical continuity;
- organizational continuity;
- inheritance of international relationships;
- transfer of archives/property;
- formal corporate legal succession.

Those are not identical.

### Production rule

Unless charter/legal acts specify corporate succession, prefer:
> `федеративная структура, созданная для сохранения связи между национальными союзами бывшего СССР`.

Avoid bare:
> `юридический правопреемник ВСЕХБ`.

unless legally sourced.

---

## 14. Russian national union ≠ Euro-Asian Federation

The Russian Baptist union is a national body.

The Euro-Asian Federation is a transnational association of national unions.

Their creation/reorganization may share leaders/history, but they occupy different organizational levels.

Bad diagram:

```text
ВСЕХБ → Российский союз → ЕАФ
```

Better:

```text
all-Union Union EHB
       ↓ state fragmentation
national unions (Russia / Ukraine / etc.)
       ↘   ↓   ↙
  Euro-Asian federation connection
```

# 22. Current institutional verdict

The end of the Soviet Baptist all-Union system was not a single collapse event.

The controlled story is:

> **The church had already rewritten its all-Union constitution in February 1990. The legal environment changed in October. The state then fragmented in 1991, and church bodies in sovereign republics acquired national independence. Leaders still sought a common connection in December 1991. In 1992 that connection took a federation form whose exact congress dates still require primary-page control. At the same time, the state began legally naming religiously motivated Soviet coercion as political repression.**

That layered sequence should replace every `1991 — ВСЕХБ распался` shortcut.
