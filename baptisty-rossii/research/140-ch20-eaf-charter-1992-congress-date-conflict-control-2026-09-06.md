## 2. Federation is an association of independent unions

Charter §1.1 states, in substance, that the Euro-Asian Federation of EHB Unions:
- is a religious association;
- was created through the free will of **independent EHB unions**;
- those unions voluntarily united for fraternal fellowship and joint work;
- member unions are governed by **their own charters**.

### Canonical consequence

This closes the organizational-level distinction:

```text
national / independent unions
           ↓ voluntary association
Euro-Asian Federation
```

It rejects the bad succession diagram:

```text
ВСЕХБ → Российский союз → ЕАФ
```

because the EAF charter itself presents a federation **of independent unions**, not a renamed Russian national union.

Safe production wording:
> `Евро-Азиатская федерация была создана как добровольное объединение самостоятельных союзов ЕХБ для братского общения и совместного труда.`

Do not upgrade this into a corporate-law succession statement unless separate legal acts establish that.

---

## 3. Member-level autonomy is explicit

The clause that member unions operate under their **own charters** is important.

It means the post-Soviet model is not merely geographic decentralization inside one all-Union hierarchy.

Analytical model:

```text
USSR all-Union church structure
        ↓ political + legal fragmentation
independent republican / national unions
        ↓ voluntary federation layer
EAF
```

This is stronger than saying only that the USSR had collapsed.

The church constitution itself records the new federative logic.

# C. SUCCESSION LANGUAGE — STRONGER CONTROL

## 5. `Правопреемник ВСЕХБ` remains meaning-sensitive

Later histories often call EAF the successor / legal successor of ВСЕХБ or Union EHB.

The charter directly proves:
- federation of independent unions;
- its own charter;
- state registration as an association.

It does **not by itself** prove:
- universal transfer of all property from former ВСЕХБ;
- automatic transfer of every legal obligation;
- identity with the Russian national union;
- uninterrupted single corporate legal person from 1944 to 1993.

Therefore:

### Approved general historical wording
> `ЕАФ стала федеративной формой сохранения связи между самостоятельными союзами ЕХБ бывшего СССР.`

### Wording requiring separate legal evidence
> `ЕАФ является юридическим правопреемником ВСЕХБ во всех имущественных и корпоративных отношениях.`

Status of the second formula: **HOLD**.
