## 0. Visual principle

Chapter 15 must not look like a generic Soviet-repression article.

Reject as hero/default:
- prison bars;
- barbed wire silhouettes;
- anonymous NKVD corridor;
- red-black hammer-and-sickle montage;
- AI interrogation room;
- fake mugshot collage;
- generic Gulag landscape with no Baptist identity.

The archive is strong enough to build the chapter from **actual congregations, choirs, leaders, families, buildings, prison notes and case photographs**.

Preferred visual grammar:

> **living congregation → institution being closed/displaced → family/leader under pressure → actual police/legal document → named case photographs → congregation on the eve of arrests → survival/relocation**.

This keeps the chapter about people and church continuity, not about aestheticized terror.
