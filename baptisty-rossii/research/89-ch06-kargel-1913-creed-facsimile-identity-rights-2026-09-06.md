## Why this matters to Chapter 6

This is a self-authored primary theological object. It should carry more documentary weight than a generic portrait because it lets the chapter show Kargel's own confessional language rather than only describe him biographically.

The facsimile should not be used to imply that the 1913 text was already the confession of every Russian Baptist or the future ВСЕХБ. Later reception/official adoption is a separate historical layer.

## Later reception guard

A 1966 `Братский Вестник` source says the ВСЕХБ leadership proposed Kargel's 1913 confession to the All-Union congress and explained the choice in terms of brevity/clarity and Kargel's standing among both Baptists and Evangelical Christians.

That later reception is useful for a short legacy epilogue or Chapter 17/18 cross-reference, but it must not be projected backwards as the 1913 purpose or status of the brochure.
