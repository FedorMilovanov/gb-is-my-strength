# 119. Chapter 12 — 1924 Bible-course duration and institutional-label resolution

Дата прохода: 2026-09-06  
Authority: `docs/BAPTISTY-ROSSII-BOOK-AUTHORITY-V2.md`  
Статус: **JOINT EXPERIMENT CLOSED / EXACT DURATION HOLD**

## 0. Conflict

Three later/contemporary description patterns currently coexist for the 1924 Leningrad training program:

1. `Братский Вестник` №3, 1957 retrospectively describes **одногодичные объединённые курсы ЕХБ** in Leningrad in 1924.
2. Later ECB histories/Master captions often describe the 1924 program as **девятимесячные** courses.
3. *Missionary Review of the World* 1925 indexed page captions `Leningrad Bible School of Evangelical Christians`, principal I. S. Prokhanov, **term January 1 to July 1, 1924**, with 37 pupils reported in accompanying text.

These descriptions cannot be silently treated as identical duration evidence.

---

## 1. What is closed

The following is safe:
- a substantial Bible/preacher-training program operated in Leningrad in 1924;
- Baptist participation is independently supported by the Walter Oliver Lewis finding aid, including a 3 Apr 1924 report from the Committee of All-Russia's Baptist Union on Bible courses in Leningrad;
- later Baptist internal history explicitly remembers the 1924 course as a joint EC/Baptist experiment;
- Prokhanov was a central institutional/teaching figure;
- the experiment did not become one durable common denominational school.

Status: **CLOSED AT NARRATIVE LEVEL**.

---

## 2. What remains open

Do not state unqualified:
- `the course lasted exactly one year`;
- `the course lasted exactly nine months`;
- `the full course ran only Jan 1–Jul 1`;
- `37 was the complete total enrollment for every 1924 training phase`.

Possible explanations that require documents rather than inference:
- MRW may describe one term within a longer instructional cycle;
- Russian `девятимесячные` tradition may include preparatory/final periods outside the photographed term;
- `одногодичные` may be a later categorical label rather than literal twelve-month duration;
- later captions may have conflated 1924 and 1925 cohorts.

No one of these explanations is currently canonical.

---

## 3. Institutional label control

Contemporary MRW caption uses:
> `Leningrad Bible School of Evangelical Christians`

Later Baptist history uses:
> `объединенные ... курсы ЕХБ`

These are not necessarily contradictory:
- host/administrative identity may have been Evangelical Christian;
- student participation/cooperation may have included Baptists.

Book wording until primary administration papers are received:
> `в 1924 году в Ленинграде действовал совместный учебный эксперимент евангельских христиан и баптистов, организованный в институциональной среде евангельских христиан.`

Avoid turning `ЕХБ` in a retrospective source into proof that a formally merged EХБ denomination already existed in 1924.

---

## 4. P0 documents that can close duration

1. Walter Oliver Lewis Collection — 3 Apr 1924 Committee of All-Russia Baptist Union report on Bible courses in Leningrad.
2. course timetable/program/catalog for 1924.
3. student roster with enrollment/graduation dates.
4. MRW 1925 page-image receipt and exact article pagination/context.
5. `Христианин` / other Russian contemporary notices announcing opening and completion.
6. Jan 1925 Baptist Educational Commission correspondence referring back to the 1924 course.

---

## 5. Production rule

Until those documents are received:
- use `1924 совместные курсы / совместный учебный эксперимент`;
- do not put an exact duration in a headline, timeline chip or machine-readable date range;
- if duration variants matter, mention them only in scholarly note;
- do not use `1 Jan–1 Jul` as a universal course date range merely because it appears in one contemporary English-language caption.

Current verdict: **JOINT COURSE FACT STRONG / HOST LABEL CALIBRATED / EXACT DURATION UNRESOLVED / NO SILENT HARMONIZATION**.
