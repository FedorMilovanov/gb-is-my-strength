## 0. Why this file exists

A weak repression narrative tends to flatten all state action into one sentence:

> `НКВД арестовало и расстреляло баптистов.`

That destroys historical precision.

Chapter 15 must distinguish at least:
1. **administrative closure / deregistration / property transfer**;
2. **OGPU/NKVD investigative arrest and exile/camp sentence**;
3. **Special Meeting / Collegium mechanisms**;
4. **regional troika mass-operation sentences**;
5. **Military Collegium of the USSR Supreme Court**;
6. later **rehabilitation review**.

The same person can pass through more than one mechanism over several years.

Rule:
> **A shared outcome does not imply a shared legal/procedural route.**

---

## 1. Administrative closure is not a criminal conviction

Examples:
- House of Gospel closure/deregistration and transfer to `Электроаппарат`;
- Moscow Baptist Union property confiscation;
- loss of prayer premises / registration.

These must not be described with criminal-case vocabulary unless an actual criminal case is involved.

Safe verbs:
- `закрыли`;
- `сняли с регистрации`;
- `передали здание`;
- `конфисковали / изъяли`, when exact property source supports it.

Do not silently upgrade:
> `община была снята с регистрации` → `члены общины были осуждены`.

---

## 2. Arrest does not equal sentence

Every named-case timeline should carry separate fields:
- arrest date;
- investigating body;
- accusation/article;
- sentencing body;
- sentencing date;
- sentence;
- execution/release date;
- rehabilitation body/date;
- archival file.

This is especially important for P. Ya. Vins, whose 1936–1937 sequence includes arrest, investigation, release, re-arrest, troika sentence and execution.

# A. ORDER No.00447

## 3. What Order 00447 proves

Primary document, 30 Jul 1937:
- creates/organizes a mass operation;
- names anti-Soviet operational contingents;
- explicitly includes `sectarian activists` and `churchmen` in the vocabulary of targeted categories;
- establishes first/second categories;
- assigns regional troikas a central role;
- specifies operation-start dates;
- controls limits/quotas and reporting.

It proves that the Great Terror apparatus had an explicit religious/sectarian category.

---

## 4. What Order 00447 does NOT prove

By itself it does not prove:
- that a particular Baptist was arrested under this order;
- that every religious case was part of the mass operation;
- that every troika sentence in 1937 was an 00447 sentence;
- that every execution of a Baptist in 1937–1939 was a first-category 00447 execution;
- that Military Collegium cases in 1939 were just delayed 00447 troika cases.

Case linkage needs:
- operational file/protocol;
- troika protocol context;
- case metadata explicitly tying the person to the operation;
- or reliable documentary scholarship making that connection from archives.

---

## 5. First vs second category

Order 00447:
- first category → arrest + troika review + execution;
- second category → arrest + 8–10 years camp, with prison variant for especially dangerous cases.

Production caution:
> `first category` is a technical operational category, not a synonym for every death sentence in Stalin-era repression.

A Military Collegium death sentence in 1939 should not be labelled `первая категория` without its own execution-list/case context.

# C. PROCEDURAL VOCABULARY CONTROL

## 15. `Тройка`

Use only when the source names a troika.

Do not use as generic synonym for:
- OSO;
- Collegium OGPU;
- Military Collegium Supreme Court;
- people's court;
- local executive committee;
- administrative commission.

---

## 16. `Особое совещание (ОСО)`

Use only with exact institutional/date wording from the applicable period/source.

Do not confuse with `Особая тройка`.

The names are similar in prose but procedurally different.

---

## 17. `Военная коллегия Верховного суда СССР`

This is a judicial body distinct from regional NKVD troikas.

For 1939 Odintsov/Datsko endpoints:
- name the Military Collegium explicitly;
- if execution-list approval/Politburo list is later verified, distinguish political approval/listing from the formal court sentence.

---

## 18. `арестован`, `осужден`, `расстрелян`

Never compress into one date unless the source proves same-day sequence.

Example:
- Datsko: sentence 15 Apr 1939; execution 16 Apr.
- Odintsov: sentence and execution both 7 Mar 1939 in memorial entry.
- Vins: troika 23 Aug; execution 26 Aug.

---

## 19. `без права переписки`

Do not automatically interpret every family report of `10 лет без права переписки` as a documented euphemism for immediate execution in that individual case.

Need:
- actual sentence/execution record;
- later rehabilitation/memorial data.

This matters for A. P. Kostyukov and similar family-memory cases.

---

# D. ACCUSATION LANGUAGE

## 20. State accusation is not historical narrator voice

When a case says:
- `counter-revolutionary agitation`;
- `anti-Soviet sectarian group`;
- `espionage`;
- `fascist organization`;
- `illegal Baptist cadres`;

write it as accusation/state characterization, not as established fact.

Preferred syntax:
> `В деле его обвиняли в...`

or:
> `следствие квалифицировало...`

Avoid:
> `он руководил контрреволюционной организацией...`

unless independent evidence establishes that.

---

## 21. Rehabilitation is analytically important

Where rehabilitation record explicitly says:
- absence of corpus delicti;
- lack of evidence;
- cancellation of troika sentence;

this should be included, not buried in a footnote.

P. Ya. Vins is especially strong because the rehabilitation protest says the case lacked proof of anti-Soviet activity.

This gives the chapter a documentary way to evaluate the accusation without relying solely on modern moral commentary.

## 24. Final control verdict

The book can say with primary confidence that Order 00447 explicitly brought `sectarian activists` and `churchmen` into the Great Terror's operational vocabulary.

The book **cannot** use that fact as a shortcut around individual case history.

The stronger result is more disturbing precisely because it is more exact:
- church buildings could be lost administratively;
- Union leaders could first be removed by prison/exile sentences;
- prisoners could later be reprocessed by special troikas;
- regional ministers could receive troika death sentences;
- other national leaders could end before the Military Collegium in 1939.

That is a system of overlapping repression mechanisms, not one simplistic decree-to-execution pipeline.
