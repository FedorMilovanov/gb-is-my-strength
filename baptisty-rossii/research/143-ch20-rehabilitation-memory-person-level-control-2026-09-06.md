## 0. Why this file exists

Chapter 20 already controls the Russian law of **18 Oct 1991 No.1761-I** as a state-level shift in the legal language of political repression, including repression on grounds of religious convictions.

That does **not** mean:

> `в 1991 году советские баптисты были реабилитированы`.

Individual cases moved on different timelines. Some had been rehabilitated decades earlier; others remained obscure to their families even after a legal rehabilitation; memorial databases created later can themselves contain conflicting metadata.

This file therefore separates four evidence layers:

```text
legal rehabilitation decision
        ≠
family notification / knowledge
        ≠
late family memory document
        ≠
modern memorial database record
```

# A. 1991 LAW — FRAMEWORK ONLY

## 1. What Law No.1761-I does prove

RSFSR Law No.1761-I, **18 Oct 1991**, is A1 evidence that the post-Soviet state formally recognized political repression, including repression connected with **religious convictions**.

This is important for Chapter 20 because it changes the public/legal vocabulary surrounding earlier persecution.

### It does not prove

- that every Baptist victim was rehabilitated on 18 Oct 1991;
- that all earlier sentences were automatically cancelled by one bulk act;
- that families immediately received accurate death information;
- that every modern memorial database is a direct copy of an individual rehabilitation act.

# B. PETER YAKOVLEVICH VINS — REHABILITATION BEFORE 1991

## 2. Case identity

Peter Ya. Vins:
- born 1898;
- Baptist leader / father of Georgi Vins;
- arrested **26 Apr 1936** in the final case chain;
- sentenced **23 Aug 1937** by troika of the Omsk UNKVD under Arts.58-10-11;
- executed **26 Aug 1937** in Omsk;
- archival/case identifier in the Omsk memory-book chain: **P-663**.

Strong source:
- Omsk Region Book of Memory `Забвению не подлежит`, vol.2.

---

## 3. Rehabilitation

The Omsk memory-book entry states:
- rehabilitation date: **20 Dec 1963**;
- body: **Presidium of the Omsk Regional Court**;
- ground: **absence of corpus delicti / состава преступления**.

This is repeated in multiple later biographies, but the Book of Memory is the controlling derivative memorial source because it gives the case identifier and legal body.

### Current state

```text
rehabilitation date/body/ground = STRONG
original court rehabilitation ruling = P0
case P-663 = STRONG
```

### Narrative consequence

Peter Vins is an excellent counterexample to the false formula:
> `реабилитация баптистских жертв началась только после закона 1991 года`.

His legal rehabilitation predates the 1991 framework by almost three decades.

---

## 4. Rehabilitation did not equal truthful family knowledge

Later Vins family history records a separate problem: the family was for years given false/partial information about Peter Vins's fate and only much later gained access to the real execution record/case history.

This is a critical Chapter 20 distinction:

```text
state legal rehabilitation
        ≠
family access to truth
```

Do not write:
> `в 1963 семья наконец узнала всю правду`.

unless the exact notification file is controlled.

The chapter can instead say:
> `юридическая реабилитация могла предшествовать полноценному восстановлению биографической правды для семьи.`

## 9. 1988 family letter is a memory source, not a rehabilitation act

# E. THREE DIFFERENT MEMORY CLOCKS

## 11. Legal, family and public clocks

These cases show at least three chronologies running independently.

### Legal clock
When a sentence/case was officially annulled or the person rehabilitated.

### Family clock
When relatives learned the actual fate and obtained enough information to reconstruct the person's story.

### Public-memory clock
When the story entered:
- newspapers;
- memorial books;
- electronic databases;
- museum/archive collections;
- public commemorative practice.

A fourth clock can also exist:

### Archival-access clock
When the investigative/re-habilitation file became accessible to family/researchers.

Chapter 20 should narrate these separately.

# G. 1991 LAW — CORRECT NARRATIVE ROLE

## 13. What changes in 1991

The value of Law No.1761-I is not that it begins every rehabilitation.

Its stronger narrative role is:
- a broad legal recognition of political repression as state injustice;
- explicit inclusion of religious conviction among the grounds;
- creation/normalization of a post-Soviet legal-memory framework;
- a new public environment in which older individual rehabilitations, family memories and newly accessible archives could be integrated.

Preferred prose:
> `К 1991 году часть баптистских жертв уже была юридически реабилитирована десятилетия назад. Новый закон изменил не одну дату в каждом деле, а сам государственный язык памяти: репрессии по религиозным убеждениям были названы формой политической репрессии.`

This is more accurate than:
> `В 1991 году государство реабилитировало репрессированных баптистов.`

# 17. Current verdict

The person-level evidence confirms that **1991 is a framework/memory turning point, not a universal rehabilitation date**.

The strongest contrast is already documentary:

```text
1958 / 1963 — individual legal rehabilitations
        ↓
1988 — family memory seeks public voice
        ↓
1991 — broad post-Soviet legal recognition of religiously motivated political repression
        ↓
```

That is a more accurate ending to the Soviet-night narrative than treating one 1991 law as a reset button.
