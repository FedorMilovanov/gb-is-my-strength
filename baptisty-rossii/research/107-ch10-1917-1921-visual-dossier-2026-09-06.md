## 0. Visual thesis

Chapter 10 must not be illustrated as a generic Russian-Revolution chapter. Its documentary sequence should show the evangelical field itself changing:

1. public freedom becomes visible in 1917;
2. Baptist/Evangelical institutions rebuild and speak publicly;
3. mission continues into Civil War conditions;
4. the Soviet legal order appears in documents and periodicals;
5. 1920–1921 combine growth, congresses, unity attempts and coercive intervention.

No AI historical reconstruction. No generic revolution crowds, Lenin portraits, red flags or stock church photographs unless a specific documentary function is proven.
