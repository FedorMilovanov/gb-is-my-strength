## 0. Editorial thesis

Chapter 15 must not become either:

> `1937: Сталин начал расстреливать баптистов`

or

> `после 1929 все церкви сразу исчезли`.

The stronger documentary architecture is:

> **1930–1935: the movement loses buildings, press, schools, regional links and leadership capacity → congregations continue, relocate, fragment or go semi-underground → 1937–1938: the Great Terror gives the police a mass-operation framework that explicitly includes “sectarian activists” and “churchmen” among targeted anti-Soviet categories → named Baptist cases show how religious association, illegal meetings, pacifism, contacts and aid networks could be rewritten as counter-revolutionary activity → families and small congregational networks preserve continuity even when legal institutional structures are destroyed.**

Chapter 15 ends before the wartime policy turn becomes the principal subject. The 1941–1944 bridge belongs to Chapter 16.

# PART II — 1937–1938: Great Terror mechanism

## 8. NKVD Operational Order No.00447 — primary mechanism CLOSED

Document:
- NKVD USSR Operational Order **No.00447**;
- date: **30 Jul 1937**;
- subject: operation against former kulaks, criminals and `other anti-Soviet elements`.

Primary/publication controls recovered:
- IstMat documentary publication;
- archival identity given as `ЦА ФСБ РФ. Ф.66. Оп.5. Д.2. Л.155–174`, original with Yezhov signature;
- another documentary publication points to `АП РФ. Ф.3. Оп.58. Д.212. Л.59–78` as an original copy/provenance line.

### 8.1. Religious/sektant vocabulary
The order's opening states that significant numbers of previously repressed `churchmen` and `sectarians` had settled in the countryside.

Its enumerated contingent explicitly includes:
- most active anti-Soviet elements among `sectarian activists` and `churchmen` in prisons, camps, labor settlements and colonies.

This is enough to establish:
> religious/sectarian status was explicitly present in the operational vocabulary of the Great Terror.

It is **not** enough to establish:
> every Baptist arrested in 1937 was processed under Order 00447.

Each named case requires its own file/sentencing body/date.

### 8.2. First and second categories
Order 00447 divides targets into:
- **first category** — immediate arrest and, after troika review, execution;
- **second category** — arrest and 8–10 years in camps, with especially dangerous cases sent to prison for the same term by troika decision.

### 8.3. Troika procedure
The order establishes:
- regional/republican troikas;
- review of materials on each arrested person or group;
- protocols recording verdicts;
- protocol/extract as basis for execution of sentence.

### 8.4. Families
The order says families were *generally* not automatically repressed, but defines exceptions and orders systematic registration/surveillance of families of first/second-category convicts.

Editorial consequence:
> do not state that Order 00447 automatically sentenced every wife/child of a Baptist prisoner; family repression must be shown from the applicable specific order/case/administrative act.

Status: **A1 MECHANISM CLOSED**.

## 9. Operation dates

Order 00447 directed commencement:
- **5 Aug 1937** generally;
- **10 Aug** in Turkmen, Tajik, Uzbek and Kyrgyz SSRs;
- **15 Aug** in East Siberian Oblast, Krasnoyarsk and Far Eastern regions.

This matters when correlating a local Baptist case with the mass-operation mechanism.

Rule:
> an arrest before the regional operation start date is not automatically an 00447 arrest merely because the sentence came later.
