## 0. Why this file exists

The Vins chronology is often compressed into one sentence:

> `27 апреля 1979 года Винса лишили гражданства и выслали, а в 1990 году Горбачёв вернул ему гражданство.`

That sentence mixes at least three legally different events:

1. the Soviet legal act depriving citizenship and ordering expulsion;
2. the physical removal/exchange;
3. the 1990 legal cancellation of the deprivation act.

The exact documentary chain can now be separated.

# A. 1979 ACT — DATE AND TITLE

## 1. Strongest current primary identification

The appendix to **USSR President Decree No.568 of 15 Aug 1990** lists the earlier citizenship-deprivation decrees being cancelled.

Item 9 identifies Vins's act as:

- issuing body: **Presidium of the Supreme Soviet of the USSR**;
- date: **24 Apr 1979**;
- title: `О лишении гражданства СССР и выдворении из пределов СССР Винса Г. П.`

This is a primary state act identifying the exact earlier act that it cancels.

### Correction

Therefore:

```text
24 Apr 1979 = legal act
27 Apr 1979 = physical expulsion / exchange
```

Do not use `27 Apr 1979` as the date of the deprivation decree.

---

## 2. Why this distinction matters

The three-day difference is historically meaningful.

It prevents the narrative from making the exchange itself the legal instrument.

Correct causal sequence:

```text
24 Apr — Presidium decree deprives citizenship + orders expulsion
        ↓
23–25 Apr — U.S.–Soviet exchange package finalized in diplomatic channel
        ↓
27 Apr — physical removal from USSR / exchange completed
```

The diplomatic and domestic legal tracks converge, but they are not the same document.

# B. 27 APR 1979 — PHYSICAL REMOVAL / EXCHANGE

## 3. Current control

Official U.S. records and later Soviet/KGB-related documentary publication support the exchange on **27 Apr 1979**.

The five Soviet prisoners were:
- Georgi P. Vins;
- Aleksandr Ginzburg;

They were exchanged for:

> `27 апреля Президиум Верховного Совета лишил его гражданства.`

# C. 1990 — THE ACT IS CANCELLED

## 4. Decree No.568

Primary state act:

- issuer: **President of the USSR**;
- date: **15 Aug 1990**;
- number: **568**;
- title: `Об отмене Указов Президиума Верховного Совета СССР о лишении гражданства СССР некоторых лиц, проживающих вне пределов СССР`;
- published: `Ведомости СНД СССР и ВС СССР`, 22 Aug 1990, No.34, art.652.

The decree orders:
1. cancellation of listed citizenship-deprivation decrees adopted in 1966–1988;
2. Ministry of Foreign Affairs notification of affected persons abroad;
3. issuance, **at their request**, of USSR passports;
4. analogous Interior Ministry work when those persons are in USSR territory;
5. entry into force **from the moment of signing**.

Item 9 of its appendix is the Vins 24 Apr 1979 decree.

### Current verdict

**15 Aug 1990 cancellation = A1 CLOSED.**

---

## 5. Legal wording matters

The primary act does not phrase the operation as a new personalized decree:

> `восстановить в гражданстве Винса Г. П.`

Instead it:
- cancels the earlier deprivation decree;
- provides passport issuance on request.

This is distinct from other 1990 citizenship acts that explicitly say `восстановить в гражданстве` for named individuals.

### Production wording

Preferred:
> `15 августа 1990 года указ Президента СССР №568 отменил указ 1979 года о лишении Винса гражданства и выдворении; МИДу и МВД было поручено обеспечить выдачу советских паспортов лицам, которых касалась отмена, по их желанию.`

Acceptable short form in non-legal prose:
> `в августе 1990 года лишение советского гражданства Винса было отменено.`

Avoid unless explaining the simplification:
> `Горбачёв просто вернул Винсу гражданство отдельным персональным указом.`

# D. `EXILE LIFTED` ≠ PRECISE LEGAL DESCRIPTION

## 6. Archive biography wording is now superseded for the legal claim

A later Vins archive finding aid says his `exile was lifted` in 1990.

That remains useful biographical shorthand, but it is less precise than Decree No.568.

The state instrument establishes:
- annulment of the **citizenship-deprivation and expulsion decree**;
- passport consequences.

It does not use the category `ссылка` as the operative legal term for this 1979–1990 status.

Therefore Chapter 20 should not describe 1979–1990 as though Vins remained under a continuing Soviet criminal-law `ссылка` sentence abroad.

The 1979 act was **citizenship deprivation + expulsion**, layered on top of the earlier criminal sentence trajectory.
