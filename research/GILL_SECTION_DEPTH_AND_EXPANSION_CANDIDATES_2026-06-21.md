# Gill section depth and expansion candidates — 2026-06-21

**Goal:** if Gill is not actually truncated, where does it nevertheless *feel* smaller or less massive than it should?  
**Method:** section-level density scan of current HTML bodies by H2 block.

---

## 1. Main conclusion

The **trilogy itself is not small**.

What may create the impression of insufficient scale is more likely:
1. some **highly uneven section weight** inside the parts;
2. the fact that **context** and **spravochnik** pages are structurally lighter than the trilogy;
3. several H2 blocks that function more like stubs / signposts than like fully developed essay units.

So the question shifts from:
- “did we lose paragraphs?”

to:
- “which sections are under-expanded relative to the ambition of the Gill world?”

## 8. Final verdict

> The Gill corpus does not currently show evidence of large text loss. The more plausible issue is uneven section density: the main trilogy remains large, while the historical-context page and the spravochnik contain several objectively short H2 blocks that can make the wider Gill world feel smaller or less “book-like” than intended.
