## 1. Why there is no new numbered dossier

Part VII is a positive pastoral-theology manuscript, not a contemporary-case article. Its governing authority is Scripture. Wave 10 is a supporting positive/accountability corpus; it is not imported into the reader as a new roster of named cases.

The source task for this Part is therefore **claim classification**, not another broad research marathon.

This receipt confirms that reader claims are kept within four distinguishable levels:

1. **TEXT** — what the cited biblical text directly establishes;
2. **CANONICAL / CONFESSIONAL INFERENCE** — synthesis across Scripture or explicitly marked Baptist confessional theology;
3. **PRUDENTIAL PASTORAL / GOVERNANCE APPLICATION** — useful controls and diagnostic questions that are not inspired procedural code;
4. **SAFETY / JURISDICTION APPLICATION** — protective action whose concrete form depends on risk, role and applicable law.

## 2. Scope

This pass verifies:

- pastoral authority under Christ and Scripture;
- character qualifications in 1 Timothy 3 and Titus 1;
- non-domineering shepherding in 1 Peter 5 / Matthew 20;
- real church leadership without administrative infallibility;
- the six-sphere authority model as a canonical/confessional/pastoral model, not six inspired lexical categories;

- create a legal safeguarding protocol;
