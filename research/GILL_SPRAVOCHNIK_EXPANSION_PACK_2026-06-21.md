# Gill spravochnik expansion pack — 2026-06-21

**Goal:** prepare a future enrichment plan for `articles/dzhon-gill-spravochnik/index.html` while live HTML remains untouched during active refactor.

---

## 1. Diagnosis

The spravochnik is not broken. It does its current job.

But relative to the owner’s likely long-term ambition — a premium, book-like Gill world — it is still too skeletal in several strategic places.

This is not a word-count crisis. It is a **function crisis**:
- the page could become a true research console,
- but at present several sections are still too compressed.

---

## 2. The five most underdeveloped modules

## Module 1 — Масштаб корпуса

Current issue:
- too short to communicate scale memorably.

What it needs:
- one strong line about corpus size / labor / breadth;
- one witness-line about how later readers perceived the scale.

Best source candidates:
- Reformed Reader summary
- Baptist Encyclopedia
- Rippon-derived later witnesses

---

## Module 2 — Structure of Body of Divinity

Current issue:
- this should be one of the most valuable parts of the reference page,
- but it is currently under-articulated.

What it needs:
- why Gill wrote it after finishing Bible exposition,
- doctrine first, practice second,
- but with both joined together,
- and explicitly for the instruction of his people.

Best source candidates:
- Body of Divinity intro (as preserved in later transcript)
- Rippon-style summary

---

## Module 3 — Network of influence and heirs

Current issue:
- too short for its strategic importance.

What it needs:
- clearer afterlife map,
- transatlantic or denominational reception,
- the role of Gill in later strict/particular Baptist memory,
- maybe later evangelical discomfort with him as well.

This section can become one of the best “bridge” modules between biography and legacy.

---

## Module 4 — Controversy map

Current issue:
- right now too concise for a reader who wants to understand why Gill remains contested.

What it needs:
- short controversy list:
  - high Calvinism,
  - election,
  - duty-faith questions,
  - Trinitarian battles,
  - rabbinic usage,
  - reading him today.

This should be written as a map, not a fight-club.

---

## Module 5 — Reading paths / “what next?”

Current issue:
- the current “read further” logic is too thin.

What it needs:
- structured reading paths:
  - beginner path,
  - doctrinal path,
  - historical path,
  - anti-Arian / Trinity path,
  - Bible commentary path.

This can make the spravochnik feel like a real editorial center.
