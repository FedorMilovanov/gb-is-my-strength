# Gill quote bank v2 — direct excerpts and future placement map

**Date:** 2026-06-21  
**Mode:** research-only; built to be safely merged later into live article files after refactor settles.

---

## 1. What this file is for

This is the next layer after the first Gill quote bank.

Its job is to hold:
- short direct excerpts,
- exact or near-exact wording from usable sources,
- and the best future placement in the Gill corpus.

The point is to let the future Gill pages feel less like a summary *about* him and more like a world in which:
- Gill speaks,
- his friends speak,
- his memorialists speak,
- his later admirers and critics speak.

## 2. Short direct-use quotations

## Q1. The honorary doctorate line

**Text**
> “I neither sought it, nor thought it, nor bought it.”

**Source family**
- Reformed Reader summary page
- later Baptist retellings
- Baptists.net “The Life and Death of John Gill”

**Best use**
- Part I, at the Aberdeen/doctorate note
- spravochnik, in a compact personality/reputation box

**Why it works**
Tiny, vivid, memorable, and immediately human.

---

## Q2. The center of his ministry

**Text**
> “I am determined not to know anything among you, save Jesus Christ, and Him crucified.”

**Source family**
- Baptists.net “The Life and Death of John Gill”
- Reformed Reader Carter Lane excerpt

**Best use**
- Part I, where the text explains what kind of pastor he was
- Part III, to reinforce continuity between early calling and late memory

**Why it works**
This is one of the strongest atmosphere quotes in the whole available Gill set.

---

## Q3. Carter Lane opening — the mature pastoral statement

**Text**
> “We enter upon this new place of worship, by recording the name of the Lord therein; namely, by preaching the doctrines of the grace of God; of free and full salvation alone, by Jesus Christ...”

**Best use**
- Part I, in the mature-pastor section
- possibly as a trimmed pull-quote

**Function**
Shows Gill as more than a scholar: a confessional-pastoral center of gravity.

---

## Q4. How long he stayed at the same post

**Text family**
> “a period of upwards of fifty-one years”

and in memorial style:
> “for more than fifty years”

**Sources**
- Baptists.net “The Life and Death of John Gill”
- Reformed Reader summary
- memorial/epitaph tradition

**Best use**
- Part I and Part III

**Function**
This is a crucial scale-marker. It gives weight, continuity, and settledness.

## Q5. On the scale of his theological labor

**Text family**
> “the last of more than ten thousand!”

This refers to the famous note about the sheets/pages and his labor.

**Best use**
- Part II (scholarship and corpus scale)
- spravochnik (“масштаб корпуса”)

**Function**
Excellent for turning corpus-size from a statistic into a scene.

---

## Q6. On the Body of Divinity as pulpit-born theology

**Text**
> “This work contains the substance of what he delivered from the pulpit to the people under his care...”

**Source**
- later Baptist transcript citing Rippon

**Best use**
- Part II, “богословские труды”
- spravochnik, Body of Divinity structure note

**Function**
This is especially important because it fights the false impression that Gill’s systematic work was detached from congregational life.

---

## Q7. On why he wrote the Body of Divinity

**Text**
> “Having completed an Exposition of the whole Bible ... I considered with myself what would be best next to engage in for the further instruction of the people under my care...”

and
> “Doctrine and practice should go together...”

**Source**
- transcript cited in Baptists.net 2025 article on Body of Divinity

**Best use**
- Part II
- spravochnik / structure of Body of Divinity

**Function**
This is one of the most important “missing atmosphere” quotes because it shows:
- he finished exposition,
- then turned to doctrine and practice,
- specifically for the instruction of his people.

---

## Q8. On systematic theology itself

**Text**
> “why should Divinity, the most noble science, be without a system?”

and
> “Evangelical truths are spread and scattered about in the sacred Scriptures; and to gather them together... surely cannot be disagreeable; but must be useful...”

**Source**
- same Body of Divinity introduction transcript

**Best use**
- Part II
- spravochnik

**Function**
This is ideal when the article wants to show Gill’s own voice on doctrinal architecture, not just modern commentary about him.

---

## Q9. Reputation as Hebraist

**Text family**
> “no man in the eighteenth century was as well versed in the literature and customs of the ancient Jews as John Gill.”

**Source**
- Baptist Encyclopedia entry

**Best use**
- Part II “учёный” frame
- spravochnik “масштаб корпуса” / “основные труды”

**Editorial caution**
Use with explicit attribution:
- not as objective final verdict,
- but as strong evidence of Baptist scholarly memory.

---

## Q10. Toplady-style reputation line

**Text family**
> “If any one man can be supposed to have trod the whole circle of human learning, it was Dr. Gill.”

**Source**
- Reformed Reader summary tradition

**Best use**
- Part II or spravochnik

**Function**
Again, not neutral fact, but powerful witness language.

---

## Q11. Election / high Calvinism line

**Text**
> “That nothing in time can be the cause of what was done in eternity.”

**Source**
- Reformed Reader Gill page

**Best use**
- Part II doctrinal profile
- section on supralapsarian reasoning / election logic

**Function**
This is one of the best short lines for showing Gill’s hard-edged theological precision.
