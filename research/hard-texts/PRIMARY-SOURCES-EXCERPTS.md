## 3. Ключевые цитаты из богословов

### 3.1 John Owen — *Of Indwelling Sin in Believers* (1656)

> “There is a law in the members, warring against the law of the mind... This is the great mystery of the Christian life — that there is in us a principle of grace and a principle of sin, both acting with power and efficacy.”

(Глава 1 — классическое описание Рим. 7 как опыта верующего)

### 3.2 Jay Street — TMSJ 30/2 (2019)

> “Romans 7 is an Old Covenant struggle seen through New Covenant eyes. The key is not whether the speaker is regenerate or unregenerate, but whether he is speaking from the perspective of life under the law or life under the Spirit.”

(Полный PDF: https://tms.edu/wp-content/uploads/2021/09/TMSJ-Volume-30-Number-2.pdf)

### 3.3 Thomas Schreiner — TGC (2016)

> “Romans 7:7–25 describes the experience of a person under the law, before the coming of the Spirit. The decisive evidence is the absence of the Holy Spirit in the passage.”

### 3.4 D. Martyn Lloyd-Jones

> “This is not the normal experience of the mature Christian. It is the experience of a man who is under the law and who is being brought to see his utter helplessness.”

(Из серии проповедей *Romans 7*)

### 3.5 John Calvin — Commentary on Romans

> “The Apostle does not here speak of the unregenerate man, but of the regenerate... For the law of God is agreeable to his mind, but the law of sin, which is in his members, leads him captive.”
