## 1. Breakthrough Discovery: Laodicea (September 2025)

### 1.1 Roman Council Hall (Bouleuterion)
- **Discovery:** Reported by Anadolu Agency and Fox News, Sept 2025.
- **Site:** Ancient Laodicea (near modern Denizli, Turkey).
- **Finds:** A massive Roman council hall dating to 50 BC, featuring carved **Christian symbols** added in later centuries.
- **Artifacts:**
    - A carved **cross** on a large stone block.
    - An early Christian **Chi-Rho** monogram.
    - Headless statue of Emperor Trajan.
- **Significance:** This discovery in the "lukewarm" city (Rev 3:14) provides fresh material evidence of Christian presence within the city's political and judicial hub. It underscores Laodicea's role as a major administrative center (overseeing 7-10 cities).

## 3. Topography & "Lukewarm" Context
- **Laodicea's Water:** Archaeology confirms the city received water via aqueducts from hot springs (Hierapolis) and cold springs (Colossae). By the time it reached Laodicea, it was **tepid/lukewarm**, providing a physical basis for the metaphor in Rev 3:16.
